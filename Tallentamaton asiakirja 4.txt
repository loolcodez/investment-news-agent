analyzed but low relevance: 30–90 päivää
high relevance analysis: 6–24 kuukautta

run statistics: 30–90 päivää

Those can be configurable items

DELETE FROM news_items
WHERE last_seen_at < datetime('now', '-30 days')
AND fingerprint NOT IN (
  SELECT news_fingerprint FROM analyses WHERE relevance >= 8
